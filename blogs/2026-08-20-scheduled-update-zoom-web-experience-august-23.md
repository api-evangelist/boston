On Sunday, August 23, between 12:00 AM and 12:30 AM, IS&T will be transitioning to Zoom's new web experience. Zoom is requiring that all customers transition to this new experience by the end of August, 2026. This updated experience will include a significant redesign and relocation of many Zoom features and workflows used today.
